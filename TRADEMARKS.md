# BananaV trademark policy

> **Draft.** This policy is still a proposal of the project's owner: until
> it is adopted it binds no one, and nothing in it is legal advice.

BananaV's **code** is free software under the
[GNU General Public License, version 3 or later](LICENSE): you may use,
study, change and share it under that license. This policy is about something
else: the **name "BananaV" and its logo** (the banana and the bar of a V, in
[`docs/brand`](docs/brand) and [`packaging/icons`](packaging/icons)), which
tell people that a program is the official BananaV, made by this project.

The GPL covers copyright, not trademarks; its section 7(e) lets a project
decline to grant rights to its trademarks, and BananaV does. The logo's
files are under the GPL like the rest of the repository, so they can be
shipped with BananaV, but using them **as a mark** — to name or represent a
program, a product or a service — is governed by this policy.

The marks belong to BananaV's founder and owner, GitHub user
[@acasoq-web](https://github.com/acasoq-web).

## What you may do without asking

- **Talk about BananaV**: write articles, reviews, tutorials, videos and books
  about it, using the name and the unchanged logo to refer to the project.
- **Share the official releases** unchanged, with their name and logo, for
  free or as part of a collection.
- **Package BananaV for a distribution or a store**, built from the
  unmodified source of a release, with only the changes packaging needs
  (build settings, integration with the system, security fixes), under the
  name BananaV.
- **Say that your work relates to BananaV** truthfully, in plain text:
  "a plug-in for BananaV", "tutorials for BananaV users", "based on
  BananaV".
- **Link to the project** with the unchanged logo.

## What needs written permission

- Using the name or the logo for a **modified version** of BananaV (see
  "Forks" below).
- Using "BananaV", or a name that could be mistaken for it, in the name of
  another program, product, company, domain or social account.
- Suggesting that the project endorses, sponsors or made your product,
  service or event.
- Changing the logo (colors, proportions, parts) or combining it with other
  marks.
- Selling merchandise with the name or the logo.

## Forks

Forks are welcome — that is what the GPL is for. A **modified version** that
you distribute must have **a name and a logo of its own**, different enough
not to be confused with BananaV:

- change the program's name, its window titles, its About text and its
  icons;
- change the packages' names and identifiers (executable, AppImage,
  desktop entry, settings folder), so both can be installed side by side;
- point users to your own issue tracker, website and support channels.

You may say, truthfully, that your program is "based on BananaV", and you
must keep the copyright and license notices the GPL asks for.

## Asking for permission, reporting misuse

Send a private message to a maintainer on the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/vtC7Yasz98). We are happy to say yes to uses that help
BananaV's users and community.
